MODULE 01 Mobile Tier · Citizen & Patient Enclave

Citizen Vital Enclave: Sovereign Health Profile & Instant SOS

Provides citizens with a secure, sovereign critical health summary and automated emergency SOS dispatch tied to verified national identity.

Problem Addressed

Panic during emergencies, unknown drug allergies, and unconscious victims completely unable to speak or provide vital medical history at trauma scenes.

Proposed Capabilities
  • checkEncrypted Critical Health Summary (CHS): Blood group, allergies, implants, and chronic conditions.
  • check3-Second Hold SOS Trigger: Instant panic-resistant beacon dispatching GPS coordinates to Rescue 1122.
  • checkBiometric & CNIC Lookup Authorization: Break-glass token derivation for on-scene medics.
  • checkProxy Guardianship & Family Alerts: Automatic SMS notifications with geo-tracking to verified emergency contacts.
Intended Benefit

Instant on-scene medical identification during the first 10 minutes of trauma while maintaining full personal privacy control and immutable access receipts.

MODULE 02 Ruggedized Field Tier · Rescue 1122 Terminal

Rescue 1122 Field Terminal: Sub-Second Paramedic Triage

Constructed specifically for first responders in transit vans and roadside conditions, enabling immediate clinical assessment and handoff preparation.

Problem Addressed

Paramedics operating in vibrating moving ambulances, blinding outdoor midday sun, and fragmented manual carbon-copy paper slips that delay emergency department admission.

Proposed Capabilities
  • checkDaylight High-Contrast UI: Surgical teal and crisp high-contrast cards legible in 45°C Punjab sunlight.
  • checkOffline Caching of Summaries: Zero downtime when ambulance transit travels through weak rural cellular coverage.
  • checkVoice Sitrep Transcription: Hands-free clinical dictation converting audio impressions to structured notes.
  • checkStandardized Electronic SBAR: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation packet sent with 1 tap.
Intended Benefit

Sub-second contraindication checking during IV administration and elimination of clinical handover delays upon reaching the trauma center bay.

MODULE 03 Hospital Clinical Tier · ER Triage Station

Hospital ER Triage Station: Pre-Arrival Inbound Radar

Provides emergency room trauma teams with live operational foresight, eliminating guesswork before the ambulance arrives.

Problem Addressed

Trauma surgeons, blood bank technicians, and triage teams are frequently completely blind to incoming patient conditions until the stretcher is wheeled through the entrance.

Proposed Capabilities
  • checkInbound Ambulance Telemetry Radar: Streaming SpO2, ECG trends, and accurate GPS countdown ETAs.
  • check1-Click Resuscitation Bay Reservation: Hold critical beds, crossmatch blood units, and pre-alert radiology.
  • checkAutomated Contraindication Cross-Check: Real-time alerts against active chronic prescriptions before rapid-sequence intubation.
  • checkStructured Digital Custody Sign-Off: Legal chain-of-custody transfer between paramedic crew and attending physician.
Intended Benefit

Trauma resuscitation teams are fully assembled, blood is staged, and CT slots are secured before wheels touch the ambulance unloading dock.

MODULE 04 Administration & Governance Tier · Hospital Console

Hospital Enclave Administration: Zero-Trust Governance & Diversion

Maintains the critical boundary between life-saving clinical utility and patient constitutional data privacy under strict statutory control.

Problem Addressed

Siloed hospital EMR systems, untracked staff record lookups, and unauthorized or unprovable access to patient files under the guise of fake emergency conditions.

Proposed Capabilities
  • checkRole-Based Credential Verification: Live integration concept with PM&DC licensing registries.
  • checkAutomated Trauma Diversion: Real-time ICU/resus saturation broadcast that reroutes ambulances before bottlenecks worsen.
  • checkImmutable WORM Audit Logs: Write-Once-Read-Many cryptographic audit streams for all break-glass actions.
Intended Benefit

Strict least-privilege compliance and legally defensible transparency for break-glass events, protecting hospital liability and citizen rights.

MODULE 05 Federated National Tier · Operations & Population Health

National Operations Command: Federation Telemetry

Aggregates anonymized, distributed emergency incident streams across all provinces, ICT, and AJK without centralizing sensitive medical histories.

Problem Addressed

Inability of health authorities to coordinate mass-casualty events, major highway pileups, or seasonal disease surges across municipal, district, and provincial boundaries.

Proposed Capabilities
  • checkGeospatial CAD Dispatch Monitoring: Real-time positioning and status of over 2,400 Rescue 1122 response units.
  • checkRegional Ambulance Fleet Strain Tracking: Live dispatch load heatmaps across Punjab, Sindh, KP, and Balochistan.
  • checkAggregate De-Identified Disease Trend Indicators: k-anonymity clustering algorithms detecting localized fever, cholera, or trauma surges.
Intended Benefit

Rapid inter-facility patient diversion during metropolitan hospital overload and proactive epidemiological early warning.
