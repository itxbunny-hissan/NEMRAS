Overcoming the Golden Hour Breakdown in Pakistan's Emergency Care

During road accidents, mass casualty events, and acute cardiac collapses, seconds wasted on identity lookup and allergy guesswork cost lives. NEMRAS re-engineers the triage paradigm.

The Baseline Reality

The Information Vacuum

Over 70% of trauma admissions in Pakistan reach emergency departments with zero historical background. Unconscious victims cannot communicate penicillin allergies, anticoagulation therapies, or chronic renal disease, forcing blind medical interventions.

Sovereign Architecture

Federated Sovereignty

Hospital records remain secure inside their native institutional databases. NEMRAS never consolidates lifetime medical records onto a single honey-pot database. Instead, only encrypted, cryptographically signed emergency vectors cross the wire.

Zero-Central Data Footprint 100% Federated Edge

Architectural Privacy Built for Constitutional Compliance

NEMRAS is engineered from the ground up to satisfy the rigorous privacy mandates of the Pakistan Personal Data Protection Bill (2023) and international health record standards.

shield
ISO/TS 17975 Compliant Health Record Pseudonymization
dataset SHA-256 Ledger

Every query, record fetch, and paramedic geolocation is appended to an immutable hash chain. Retrospective tampering is mathematically impossible.

emergency Dual Break-Glass

Unconscious patient access without prior consent requires two certified medical professionals to sign off on an emergency override key.

groups k-Anonymity (k ≥ 50)

National disease analytics and trauma density statistics are systematically bucketed so no individual patient identity can ever be reverse-engineered.

sms Instant Audit SMS

When a paramedic opens an emergency file, an out-of-band notification ping lands on the registered citizen's phone detailing the responder's ID and hospital.
